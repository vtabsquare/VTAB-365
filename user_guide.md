# VTAB 365 User & Administrator Guide

Welcome to VTAB Office Suite 365. This manual provides instructions on how to use the workspace platform effectively.

---

## Part 1: Employee Guide

### Logging In
1. Navigate to the VTAB 365 portal.
2. Enter your registered email address and password.
3. Check your email inbox. You will receive a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) from the system.
4. Enter the 6-digit code on the verification screen to securely access your dashboard.

### Using the Dashboard
* **Quick Access:** The top section of your dashboard contains your most-used applications. Click **Customize** to pin up to 6 of your favorite tools for instant access.
* **All Applications:** Browse the grid to see all tools provisioned for your role. You can filter by category (e.g., HR, Finance) or use the search bar at the top of the screen.
* **App Details:** Click the "info" (i) icon on any application card to see version details, publisher information, and a detailed description.
* **What's New:** Scroll down or click "What's new" in the sidebar to view the release timeline for upcoming features and recent updates.

---

## Part 2: Administrator Guide

Administrators have access to the protected **App settings** dashboard to manage the platform.

### Managing Applications
1. Click **App settings** in the sidebar.
2. Under the **Add application** tab, you can register a new tool. You must provide a unique URL slug, select the SSO protocol, and configure brand colors/logos.
3. **Visibility:** When creating or editing an app, set it to "Everyone" (visible in the main launcher) or "Administrators only" (hidden from standard employees).
4. **Toggling Status:** Go to the **Manage & visibility** tab to temporarily disable or permanently remove an application.

### Managing Users
1. Go to the **Manage users** tab.
2. You can view all registered employees. 
3. To grant a user administrative privileges, change their dropdown from "Employee" to "Administrator" and click **Save**. This action is logged securely.

### Publishing Announcements
1. Go to the **Upcoming releases** tab.
2. Fill out the form with a headline, targeted release date, and progress percentage.
3. This will immediately push a notification card to the "What's New" timeline for all users.

### Reviewing the Audit Trail
The **Audit trail** tab provides a real-time, read-only security log. It tracks:
* User Logins (Success and Failures)
* SSO App Launches
* Administrator Actions (e.g., App creation, role modifications)
